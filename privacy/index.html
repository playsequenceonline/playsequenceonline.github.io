Privacy policy

Chipline is a free card board game. We keep as little about you as we can, and we never sell it. Last updated 14 November 2026.

Playing without an account

Games against the computer and pass-and-play games run on your device. Your name, sound setting, chosen style and any saved game stay in your browser's storage and are not sent to us.

When you play online with friends, the game gives you a random guest ID so the server knows which seat is yours. The server stores the room: player names you typed, the cards, the board and the moves. Rooms are deleted automatically after two days without play.

Emoji reactions are passed live between the players in a room and are not saved.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. It only exists so your online wins can count on the leaderboard. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We use your first name to fill in your player name and to show that you are signed in. We do not post anything to your Google account, and we do not read your contacts, files or anything else.

Sign-in and accounts are handled by Supabase, the service that runs our game server.

The leaderboard

For signed-in players we store the number of online games played and won, and the player name used in your latest counted game. The leaderboard is public and shows that name with your wins, games and win rate. It never shows your email or picture. Games only count when at least two real people played.

Visitor statistics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and game modes are used, so we can improve the game. It uses cookies and collects things like your rough location, device type and pages visited. It does not tell us who you are.

Who else handles data

  • Supabase runs the game server, database and sign-in.
  • Google provides sign-in and Google Analytics.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. If you play on itch.io, itch.io hosts that copy.

We do not show ads and we do not sell or rent personal data.

Deleting your data

To sign out, use Sign out on the home screen. To have your account and leaderboard entry deleted, post a request on our itch.io page with the player name you use, and we will remove it. Guest data disappears on its own when rooms are cleaned up.

Children

Chipline is a family-friendly game. Signing in with Google is meant for people old enough to have their own Google account.

Changes

If we change how we use data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
