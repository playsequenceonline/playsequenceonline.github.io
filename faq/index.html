The basics

How many cards do you deal in Sequence?

7 cards each with 2 players, 6 with 3 or 4 players, 5 with 6 players, 4 with 8 or 9 players, and 3 with 10 or 12 players. With 3 teams it goes by total players: 3 players get 6 cards, 6 get 5, 9 get 4 and 12 get 3. See the full table.

How many cards are in a Sequence game?

104 cards: two standard 52-card decks with the jokers removed. That includes 8 jacks.

How many spaces are on a Sequence board?

100 spaces in a 10 by 10 grid. Every card except the jacks appears twice (96 spaces), and the four corners are free spaces.

How many players can play Sequence?

2 to 12, as long as the players split evenly into 2 or 3 sides: 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10 or 12 players.

Can you play Sequence with 5 or 7 players?

Not with the standard rules, because 5 and 7 do not split evenly into 2 or 3 teams. In Chipline you can add a computer player to make 6 or 8.

What age is Sequence for?

The boxed game is rated for ages 7 and up. Younger children can play on a team with an adult.

Winning

How many sequences do you need to win?

Two sequences when there are 2 players or 2 teams. One sequence when there are 3 players or 3 teams.

Do diagonal lines count in Sequence?

Yes. A sequence is five chips in a row across, down or diagonally.

Do the corners count as a chip?

Yes. The four corners are free spaces that count for every side, so a line through a corner needs only four of your own chips.

Can two sequences share a chip?

Yes, but only one. Your second sequence may reuse one chip from your first.

Does six in a row count as two sequences?

No. Six chips in a row is one sequence. To count as two, a longer line needs nine chips, so that the two fives share only one chip.

Jacks

What do two-eyed jacks do?

Two-eyed jacks (diamonds and clubs) are wild. Play one to place your chip on any open space.

What do one-eyed jacks do?

One-eyed jacks (spades and hearts) remove one opponent chip from the board. You do not place a chip of your own on that turn.

Can you remove a chip from a completed sequence?

No. Once a sequence is complete, its chips are locked and a one-eyed jack cannot remove them.

How many jacks are in Sequence?

Eight: four two-eyed jacks and four one-eyed jacks.

During the game

What is a dead card?

A card whose two board spaces are both covered. On your turn you may discard it, draw a replacement, and then take your normal turn. You can do this once per turn.

What happens if you forget to draw a card?

In the boxed game, if the next player has already moved, you lose the right to draw and play on with a smaller hand. In Chipline cards are drawn for you, so it cannot happen.

What happens when the deck runs out?

Shuffle the discard pile and use it as the new deck.

Can teammates talk during Sequence?

Teammates may not tell each other which cards they hold or which space to play. In the boxed game the penalty is that every player on the team discards a card of their choice.

Playing online

Can I play Sequence online for free?

Chipline is a free Sequence-style game that runs in your browser with no download or sign-up. You can play with friends on your own phones or against the computer.

Is Chipline the official Sequence game?

No. Chipline is an independent game with the same style of play: cards, chips and lines of five. It is not affiliated with the makers of Sequence.

How long does a game take?

Most games take around 15 to 30 minutes. Bigger groups and three-team games tend to be quicker, because only one line is needed.
