How a turn works

  1. Pick a card from your hand. Its open spaces light up on the board.
  2. Tap one to drop your team's chip. You draw a new card automatically.
  3. J♦ and J♣ are wild: drop a chip on any open space.
  4. J♠ and J♥ remove: flick off one opponent chip that is not in a finished line.
  5. The four star corners are free and count for every team.
  6. Two teams need two lines of five. Three teams need one.

Play a Sequence-style card board game online, free

Chipline is a free online five-in-a-row card board game, in the style of Sequence, for 2 to 12 players. Play in your browser with friends on different phones, against computer players, or pass one phone around the table. No download or sign-up.

How to play in 3 steps

  1. Each player holds a hand of cards. Play one card and drop your team's chip on a matching space on the board.
  2. Use jacks wisely: two-eyed jacks are wild, one-eyed jacks remove an opponent's chip.
  3. Make lines of five in a row across, down or diagonally. Two teams need two lines; three teams need one.

Questions players ask

Is Chipline free?

Yes. Chipline is completely free to play in your web browser, with no download and no sign-up.

How many players can play?

From 2 to 12 players: 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10 or 12. Bigger groups split into 2 or 3 teams that take turns in order.

Can I play with friends on different phones?

Yes. Tap Play with friends, start a new room and send the invite link on WhatsApp. Each friend opens the link on their own phone, types a name and joins.

Can I play against the computer?

Yes. Tap Play the computer for a one-tap game, or add computer players to any seat in an online room or a pass-and-play game.

How do you win?

Two teams need two lines of five chips. Three teams need one line. Lines can run across, down or diagonally, and the four star corners count for every team.

What do the jacks do?

Two-eyed jacks (diamonds and clubs) are wild: place a chip on any open space. One-eyed jacks (spades and hearts) remove one opponent chip that is not part of a finished line.

How online play works

  1. Send the invite link or the room code to your friends.
  2. Everyone opens it on their own phone and types a name.
  3. Seats alternate between teams, in the order shown. The host can reorder them.
  4. The host starts once there are 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10 or 12 players.
  5. Each player sees only their own cards. The server deals and checks every move.
  6. Short on people? The host can add computer players.
  7. If a turn runs out of time, the computer plays it. Miss two in a row and the computer keeps playing for you until you tap I'm back.
  8. If someone leaves mid-game, a computer takes their seat so the game goes on.
