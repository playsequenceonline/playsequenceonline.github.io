One-eyed vs two-eyed jacks in Sequence

Published 15 November 2026 · Chipline

In Sequence, the two-eyed jacks (jack of diamonds and jack of clubs) are wild: you place your chip on any free space. The one-eyed jacks (jack of spades and jack of hearts) remove one opponent chip from the board. Jacks are not printed on the board, so these are the only ways to use them.

How to tell them apart

JackEyesWhat it doesHow many
Diamonds, clubsTwo (face looks straight out)Wild: place a chip anywhere free4
Spades, heartsOne (face in profile)Remove one opponent chip4

Card art varies between decks, so if a face is hard to read, go by the suit: diamonds and clubs are wild, spades and hearts remove. In Chipline you don't need to remember: jacks in your hand are tagged Wild or Remove.

Rules that trip people up

  • A one-eyed jack takes your whole turn. You remove a chip but do not place one of your own.
  • Finished sequences are safe. A one-eyed jack cannot remove a chip that is part of a completed sequence.
  • You cannot remove a corner. Corners are free spaces, not chips.
  • The space you clear is open to everyone, including the player you removed, so remove the chip that hurts them most.
  • A jack is never a dead card. There is always somewhere to use it.

When to play each jack

Save two-eyed jacks to finish your own line or block a line of four. Save one-eyed jacks to break an opponent's line of four, ideally on a chip where two of their lines cross.

Spending either jack early is the most common beginner mistake. With 4 of each in a 104-card deck, you may only see one or two per game. Our strategy tips cover more ways to use them.

Quick questions

Which jacks are wild in Sequence?

The two-eyed jacks: the jack of diamonds and the jack of clubs. Place your chip on any free space.

Which jacks remove a chip in Sequence?

The one-eyed jacks: the jack of spades and the jack of hearts. Remove one opponent chip that is not part of a finished sequence.

Can a one-eyed jack remove a chip from a completed sequence?

No. Once five chips form a sequence they are safe for the rest of the game.
