Can you play Sequence with 3 people?

Published 15 November 2026 · Chipline

Yes, you can play Sequence with 3 people. Each player plays alone with their own colour, gets 6 cards, and the first player to make one sequence of five wins. Everything else (the jacks, the corners, dead cards) works exactly as in the normal game.

3-player rules at a glance

RuleWith 3 players
Sides3 players, each on their own
Chip colours3, one per player
Cards in each hand6
Sequences to win1
Jacks, corners, dead cardsSame as always

How the game changes with 3

A three-player game is shorter and more cut-throat than a two-player one, because a single line of five wins and every player is a threat.

  • Block the leader, not your favourite target. Whoever is closest to five is the problem, even if it means the third player gets a free turn.
  • Expect help from the other player. If you are far behind, the third player will often block the leader for you. Use that turn to build.
  • Jacks are even more valuable. With only one line needed, a two-eyed jack on a line of four ends the game.

What about 5 or 7 players?

Sequence needs equal teams, so 5, 7 and 11 do not split evenly. The usual fix is for one player to sit out each round, or to fill the gap with a computer player. In Chipline any seat can be a computer, so 5 friends can play as 3 teams of 2 with one computer teammate. See the full player table.

Quick questions

How many cards does each player get with 3 players?

Each of the 3 players gets 6 cards.

How many sequences do you need to win with 3 players?

Only one. With 3 players or 3 teams, the first side to make one sequence of five wins.

Can 3 people play Sequence in teams?

Not with only 3 people, since each team needs at least 2 players. With 6, 9 or 12 players you can play 3 teams, and each team still needs one sequence.
