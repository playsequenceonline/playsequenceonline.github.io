How to play Sequence with a big group (6 to 12 players)

Published 15 November 2026 · Chipline

Sequence works for groups of 6 to 12 by splitting into 2 or 3 equal teams that sit in alternating order. Each player gets fewer cards as the group grows: 5 cards with 6 players, 4 with 8 or 9, and 3 with 10 or 12. Two teams need two sequences to win; three teams need one.

Seating and turns

Sit so the teams alternate around the table (A, B, A, B or A, B, C, A, B, C). Turns go around in order, so no team ever plays twice in a row.

Teammates share one chip colour but may not show or describe their cards. With only 3 to 5 cards in hand, the best move is usually to extend a line a teammate started. Our strategy guide has more on team play.

Odd numbers: 5, 7 and 11

Teams must be equal, so one player can sit out each round and swap in for the next, or you can fill the missing seat with a computer player in Chipline.

Quick questions

Can 12 people play Sequence?

Yes. Play 2 teams of 6 (two sequences to win) or 3 teams of 4 (one sequence to win). Each player gets 3 cards.

How many cards does each player get with 8 players?

With 8 players, split into 2 teams of 4, and each player gets 4 cards.

Can a big group play Sequence on different phones?

Yes. In Chipline the host starts an online room and shares the link, and up to 12 players join from their own phones.
